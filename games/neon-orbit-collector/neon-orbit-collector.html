Neon Orbit Collector – Cyber Arcade Survival Game

Neon Orbit Collector is an original HTML5 arcade game designed for Neon Mini Game Hub. The player controls a fast cyber ship in a glowing orbital arena, collects energy cores, avoids rotating mines, and builds a score combo by staying alive.

Gameplay Loop

The core loop is simple and replayable: move, collect, dodge, boost, survive longer, and beat your local high score. The difficulty increases over time by spawning more hazards and increasing their speed.

Controls

Desktop: Use WASD or arrow keys to move. Press Space for a short boost. Mobile: Use the on-screen direction pad and BOOST button.

Why It Fits the Hub

The game uses the same neon palette, framed layout, sidebar control panel, background approach, localStorage score saving, and ad script hook as the attached Neon Sudoku Deluxe page.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, it runs directly in the browser with no login required.

Does it save progress?

The game stores your best score locally on your device using localStorage.

How to Play Neon Orbit Collector

Goal: Collect as many cyan energy orbs as possible while avoiding red mines.

Movement: WASD, arrow keys, or mobile direction pad.

Boost: Press Space or tap BOOST. Boost has a short cooldown.

Combo: Collect orbs quickly to increase your multiplier.

Lives: You start with 3 lives. Mines remove one life and reset the combo.

Difficulty: Higher difficulty increases mine speed and spawn pressure.

Powerups: Green slows mines, purple magnet pulls orbs, blue shield blocks one hit.

Daily Challenge: A seeded run that changes every day and saves your best daily score.

Achievements: Milestones are saved locally with localStorage.
